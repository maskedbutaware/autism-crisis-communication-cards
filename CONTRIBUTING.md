# Contributing

Contributions should make the cards easier to understand during overload.

## Language guidelines

- Keep each sentence focused on one immediate need.
- Avoid diagnosing the person or naming their experience for them.
- Do not promise safety or a specific outcome.
- Avoid idioms, motivational language and unnecessary explanation.
- Preserve the meaning when adding a translation; do not translate word for word when that sounds unnatural.

## Adding a language

Add both a printable HTML card under `cards/` and a matching JSON file under `data/`. Include the language name and locale in your pull request description. A fluent speaker should review the wording before it is merged.

Do not add tracking scripts, advertising or data collection.
